Rapido Bikepool & Intercity Ride-Share

Understanding the Market Gap, Casual Commuter Behavior, and 2 New Core Products

💡 1. The Real-World Discovery (The Market Gap)

What is happening right now?

Across metropolitan cities, thousands of bike owners ride to work alone while paying ₹100+/L for petrol. To offset this monthly expense, many riders sign up on the Rapido Captain app. But they do not want to be full-time commercial taxi drivers—they just want to share their empty seat with someone traveling to the same office tech park and split the fuel cost.

⚠️ The Core Problem Today Because the current Captain app is made for commercial taxis, it assigns random rides in different directions. The commuter host has to cancel multiple rides, negotiate over phone calls, and face penalties. Meanwhile, passengers struggle with expensive ₹250+ auto surge fares during rush hour.
🚀 The Big Business Opportunity Create a dedicated Peer-to-Peer Bikepooling service inside Rapido with two clear offerings:
1. Host a Ride (For bike owners to share their seat on their route).
2. Want to Share a Ride (For passengers to find and join matching hosts).

🏍️ 2. The Two New Host Features

Feature 1: Inside the City

Daily Commute Pool (5 km – 30 km)

  • Distance Range: 5 km to 30 km (Covers all daily office routes).
  • Automatic Pricing: Rapido's algorithm automatically sets a fair fuel-split price. No bargaining.
  • 5-Minute Live Timer: Host lists the ride 5 mins before leaving. A 5-minute countdown runs while nearby passengers select the host.
Feature 2: City to City

Highway Bike Sharing (Inter-City)

  • Advance Scheduling: Host chooses starting city and destination city; schedules ride at least 1 hour in advance.
  • Host Sets Price: The rider chooses how much to charge their co-partner for sharing the long journey.
  • Gear & Luggage Check: Host confirms spare helmet, backpack limit, and planned rest/food halts.

👥 3. The Three User Personas

Persona Who They Are How They Use Rapido
👤 Passenger Daily commuter or traveler seeking a fast, affordable ride. Enters pickup/destination, compares matching hosts by rating, bike, and price, and joins.
🏍️ Inside-City Host Bike owner traveling 5–30 km to work. Enters route, gets auto-generated fair price, starts 5-min timer, and picks up matching co-rider.
🛣️ City-to-City Host Rider traveling between two cities. Schedules trip ≥ 1 hr ahead, sets custom price, and accepts a co-traveler to split costs.
